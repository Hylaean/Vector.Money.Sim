# Outreach Plan

*Status as of July 2026, from a systematic sweep of existing channels. Companion to [proposals/README.md](../proposals/README.md) (funding) and [thesis.md](thesis.md) (content).*

## Where we start: zero

A honest audit found **no existing public outreach**: the repo has no description, no topics, no stars; hylaean.com does not resolve; the author's Medium (1 post, 2019), dev.to (0 posts), and GitHub profile make no mention of the project. This is a blank slate — no audience, but total positioning freedom.

**Naming reality check.** The exact phrase "vector money" is unclaimed by any project or paper, but bare-phrase search is dominated by clipart ("money vector" images) and crypto apps named Vector. The nearest academic prior art is *Money as a Tensor* (arXiv:2504.06286, 2025 — multidimensional but not biophysical, no simulation) and Lietaer's complementary-currency lineage (critiqued by Larue 2020). **Rule: never use the bare phrase alone.** Always pair it with distinguishing terms: *biophysical*, *agent-based*, *Doughnut Economics*, *ecological ceilings*, *Mesa*, *Hylaean*.

## The message (one breath)

> Money compresses every ecological fact into one number. We built an open testbed for money that doesn't — and we test it on software agents, LLMs, and people, in worlds where the climate holds still and worlds where it doesn't. In stable worlds, ordinary money wins. In shifting worlds, vector money halves ecological overshoot. Come try to break it.

The honesty (*"in stable worlds, ordinary money wins"*) is the differentiator. Every channel leads with the trade-off, never with advocacy.

## Voice rules

1. **Test, don't advocate.** "We don't advocate vector money; we test it" appears near the top of everything.
2. **Lead with the loss.** The static-world welfare cost is stated before the shifting-world win.
3. **Critiques by name.** Hayek, Friedman, Camera, Thaler — named, engaged, and turned into experiments. Steelmanning is the brand.
4. **Everything reproducible.** Every number in outreach is one command away from being checked; say the command.
5. **Neutral in participant-facing material.** The human experiment says "stamps" and "materials" — no climate framing (it's a measured variable later, not a confound now).
